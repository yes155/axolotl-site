What Is a Golden Albino Axolotl?

A golden albino axolotl is an albino (a/a) that carries a functional D allele at the white locus, producing a solid yellow or golden body with pink or red eyes. It is one of the most colorful standard morphs.

The a/a genotype removes all dark melanin, and the functional D allele keeps the yellow xanthophore cells working, so the remaining color is pure yellow-gold over a pale base.

A golden albino typically costs $40 to $80, similar to the other common albino morphs.

Golden Albino vs. Albino: What Is the Difference?

A golden albino is yellow or gold with a bright golden sheen, and a plain albino is white or pale cream with no distinct yellow tone. Both have pink or red eyes and gills because both lack melanin.

The difference comes from the white locus. A golden albino carries at least one functional D allele, which drives xanthophore production. A plain albino carries d/d at the white locus, so no yellow pigments develop.

The eye color is identical in both, so body color is the only way to tell them apart.

How Do You Get a Golden Albino Axolotl?

A golden albino is produced whenever an albino (a/a) inherits a functional D allele at the white locus, which happens whenever either parent contributes D.

Because both genes are inherited independently, an albino x albino cross produces plain albinos only, and a golden albino x albino cross produces each color based on the white-locus genes carried.

The table below shows the main crosses:

CrossOffspringOdds
Golden Albino x AlbinoGolden + plain albino50% each
Golden Albino x Golden AlbinoGolden + plain albino75% / 25%
Golden Albino x LeucisticGolden carrier + lucy carrier100% look wild-type

All offspring of an albino cross carry at least one a allele, so the albino genes stay in the line.

Do Golden Albino Axolotls Need Different Care?

A golden albino axolotl needs exactly the same care as any other morph, because the albino mutation changes only pigment, never health or husbandry needs.

Golden albinos have pink-red eyes that function normally in dim water, but they prefer low or moderate lighting. Like all axolotls, they need a cool 16-18C tank with a gentle filter and a carnivore diet.

Bright tanks stress the eyes, so keep lighting dim. The yellow body makes a golden albino easy to spot, which helps when checking for wounds or fungus.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a golden albino axolotl cost?

A golden albino costs $40 to $80, and well-fed adults with long gills sell for more.

Are golden albino axolotls rare?

Golden albinos are common and bred in large numbers, so they are not a rare morph.

Why is my golden albino turning orange?

A golden albino's yellow deepens to orange as it matures and fattens, which is a normal color change driven by diet and age.

Do golden albinos glow in the dark?

Golden albinos do not glow; only genetically modified GFP axolotls fluoresce under blue light.
