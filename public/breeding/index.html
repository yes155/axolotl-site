Breeding axolotls is a rewarding but demanding process. This guide walks you through conditioning your breeders, getting a successful spawn, raising hundreds of larvae, and breeding ethically with an understanding of the genetics.

Axolotl Egg and Larvae Care: A Complete Guide

Axolotl eggs hatch in about 15 days at 20°C (68°F), in under 14 days at 25°C (77°F), and in more than 20 days at 18°C (64°F). Temperature is the sole control over hatching speed.

How to Tell Axolotl Gender (Sexing Guide)

An axolotl is reliably sexable at 18 months, because until that age every juvenile looks female. Before 18 months, every axolotl shows only a small, downward-pointing cloaca.

Raising Baby Axolotls (Juveniles) to Adulthood

Newly hatched axolotl larvae eat only live baby brine shrimp for their first weeks, because they respond to movement, not smell. They ignore dead food until they are much larger.
