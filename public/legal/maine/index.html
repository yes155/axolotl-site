Are Axolotls Legal in Maine?

Maine lists the axolotl as a Category 2 Restricted Species, which means legal ownership requires a possession permit and, for out-of-state animals, an import permit. This guide covers the permits, fees, and qualifications.

Is It Legal to Own an Axolotl in Maine?

Yes. You can legally own an axolotl in Maine if you hold a Category 2 Restricted Species Wildlife Possession Permit, because Maine lists Ambystoma mexicanum as a Category 2 Restricted Species. Maine law, 12 M.R.S. Section 12152, prohibits importing or possessing wildlife without a permit unless the species is on the department's unrestricted list. The axolotl is not on that list, so the permit requirement applies.

What Permits Do You Need for an Axolotl in Maine?

Maine requires 2 permits for axolotls: a Wildlife Possession Permit and a Wildlife Importation Permit for animals brought from outside the state. Both come from the Maine Department of Inland Fisheries and Wildlife. The permits with fees:

PermitFeeValid for
Wildlife Possession Permit (Category 2)$27 application + $27 permit2 years
Wildlife Importation Permit (Category 2)$100 application + $27 permitPer import

Apply before you acquire the animal, because possession without a permit triggers a $500 surcharge if you apply late.

Who Qualifies for a Maine Category 2 Permit?

Applicants must be an exhibitor, wildlife rehabilitator, USDA-registered laboratory, accredited research facility, or an individual with demonstrated axolotl husbandry experience. The credentials require a minimum of 1 year of practical husbandry or 100 hours of experience, with a Bachelor of Science in a biological science substituting for 50 hours. Two personal references are required, and the axolotl must be of captive-bred origin.

What Happens If You Keep an Axolotl Without a Permit in Maine?

Each day without a permit is a Class E crime with a minimum fine of $50 plus twice the applicable permit fee. If you apply late after already possessing the axolotl, Maine adds a $500 surcharge on top of the application and permit fees. Maine allows a late permit in place of prosecution, but only with the surcharge.

Can I Buy an Axolotl from a Maine Pet Store?

Yes, if the store legally sells axolotls, but you must still hold your own possession permit to keep one. Maine pet stores are regulated and inspected by the Maine Department of Agriculture, Conservation and Forestry. Animals for sale in other states are not automatically lawful to bring into Maine.

Does Federal Law Affect Axolotls in Maine?

Yes. Since January 25, 2025, the genus Ambystoma is listed as injurious wildlife under the federal Lacey Act, so shipping an axolotl into Maine from another state violates federal law without a federal permit. The federal listing covers scientific, educational, medical, or zoological purposes only. This means new axolotls for Maine effectively come from in-state sources.

How Long Is a Maine Category 2 Possession Permit Valid?

A Category 2 restricted species possession permit expires 2 years from the date of issuance, and it must then be renewed. Each renewal requires a new $27 application fee and $27 permit fee. The importation permit is separate and valid per import.

Can I Import an Axolotl into Maine from Another State?

You can import an axolotl into Maine from another state only with a Wildlife Importation Permit, and since 2025 federal law additionally prohibits interstate shipping without a federal injurious wildlife permit. Both permits are required. In practice, new axolotls for Maine come from in-state breeders.

This page is a plain-English summary and is not legal advice. Contact the Maine Department of Inland Fisheries and Wildlife for current permit applications, fees, and requirements.
