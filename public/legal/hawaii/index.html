Are Axolotls Legal in Hawaii?

Hawaii does not list the axolotl as a prohibited species, but it regulates every non-domestic animal import, and the federal rule has blocked interstate axolotl shipping since 2025. This guide covers the state law, the permit steps, and the federal ban.

Is It Legal to Own an Axolotl in Hawaii?

No. You cannot legally import an axolotl into Hawaii as a pet, because importing requires a Plant Quarantine permit and the Board of Agriculture approves each unlisted-species application case by case. The axolotl is not on Hawaii's prohibited list, so a permit is technically possible, but animals on the Restricted Part B list are not allowed to be imported as pets. Federal law has blocked interstate axolotl shipping since January 25, 2025.

How Does Hawaii Regulate Axolotl Imports?

Hawaii regulates the import of every non-domestic animal under Title 4, Chapter 71, of the Hawaii Administrative Rules (HAR 4-71), enforced by the Department of Agriculture's Plant Quarantine Branch. Non-domestic animals fall into 5 categories, and each has a different import rule. Table by category:

Animal listImported as a pet?
Conditionally approvedYes, with an import permit
Restricted Part AResearch, zoos, government only
Restricted Part BNot allowed to be imported as pets
ProhibitedBanned entirely
UnlistedPermit required, case by case

Every import needs a permit before the animal arrives, and all shipments get inspected at entry.

Which Salamanders Are Prohibited in Hawaii?

Hawaii prohibits 3 salamander groups - Amphiuma, Necturus (water dogs and mudpuppies), and sirens - but not Ambystoma, the genus that includes the axolotl. Because the axolotl is not on the prohibited list, it falls into the unlisted category. Unlisted species require Board of Agriculture approval before a permit is issued.

How Do You Apply for an Axolotl Import Permit in Hawaii?

You submit Form PQ-7 to the Hawaii Department of Agriculture's Plant Quarantine Branch, including the reasons for introduction, the responsible person, the safeguard facilities, and the method of disposition. Restricted animals additionally require a site inspection and a separate possession permit before import. For unlisted species, the application goes through two advisory committee reviews and a Board of Agriculture decision.

Does Federal Law Restrict Axolotls in Hawaii?

Yes. Since January 25, 2025, the genus Ambystoma is listed as injurious wildlife under the Lacey Act, and Hawaii is explicitly included in the federal interstate shipping ban. Shipping an axolotl to Hawaii from the mainland violates federal law without a federal permit for scientific, educational, medical, or zoological purposes. The state permit alone does not authorize interstate shipment.

Are There Axolotls Already in Hawaii?

Some axolotls exist in private or institutional collections from before the current rules, but there is no legal retail trade in axolotls in Hawaii. No new axolotls can ship into the state without both state and federal permits. Buyers have no legal source for a new pet axolotl.

Who Do I Contact About Importing an Axolotl to Hawaii?

The Hawaii Department of Agriculture's Plant Quarantine Branch processes all non-domestic animal import permits, at (808) 832-0566. The branch can tell you whether a specific request is possible. Form PQ-7 is available from any Plant Quarantine office or as a fillable PDF.

This page is a plain-English summary and is not legal advice. Contact the Hawaii Department of Agriculture's Plant Quarantine Branch to confirm the current rules before planning any import.
