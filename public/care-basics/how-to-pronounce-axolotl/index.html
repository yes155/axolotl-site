How do you pronounce axolotl?

Pronounce axolotl as ACK-suh-lot-ul, with the stress on the first syllable. The word has four syllables, and English dictionaries give it as /ˈæk.sə.lɒt.əl/ in British English and /ˈæk.sə.lɑː.təl/ in American English. Cambridge and Merriam-Webster both place the primary stress on ACK.

A keeper's variant stresses the third syllable as ack-suh-LOT-ul, mirroring the Spanish word ajolote. The dictionary stress is ACK; the variant is common in the pet community.

What is the syllable breakdown?

The four syllables are ACK, suh, lot, and ul, each with a simple sound.

SyllableSoundRhyme
ACK"a" as in hat, plus "k"ack
suh"uh" as in abovesuh
lot"o" as in sock or fatherlot
ul"ul" as in labelul

Put the beats together as ACK-suh-lot-ul and put the weight on the first beat.

What is the most common mistake?

The most common mistake is stressing the wrong syllable, saying AX-oh-lotl instead of ACK-suh-lot-ul. A second common error drops the final "l" sound and says ACK-suh-lot. Both errors disappear when the stress lands on the first syllable and the "ul" ending is spoken.

Why is axolotl hard to pronounce?

The word is hard because it comes from Nahuatl, the Aztec language, and does not follow English spelling rules. The "x" makes a "ks" sound, and the "tl" cluster is foreign to English. Speakers guess from the written form, and the guess lands on the wrong syllable because Nahuatl stress rules differ from English.

What does axolotl mean?

Axolotl means "water dog," "water monster," or "water slave" in Nahuatl. The name comes from the Aztec god Xolotl, who in myth transformed into an axolotl to escape sacrifice. The root xolo-tl carries the god's name, and atl is the Nahuatl word for water.

How do you say the "tl" ending?

Say the "tl" as a quick "tul," with a soft, unstressed "u," and never as a long "tal." The final letters make the /təl/ sound, like the end of "bottle." A long, drawn-out "tal" adds a beat the word does not have.

How can I remember the pronunciation?

Remember it as ACK-suh-lot-ul, broken into two clean halves: ACK-suh and lot-ul. Practice each half, then join them. Say it slowly three times, then in a sentence: "My axolotl lives in a 20-gallon tank."

Which syllable is stressed in axolotl?

The first syllable, ACK, carries the primary stress in English. British and American dictionaries agree on the placement: /ˈæk.sə.lɒt.əl/ and /ˈæk.sə.lɑː.təl/.

Why do people pronounce axolotl wrong?

People pronounce it wrong because the Nahuatl spelling does not follow English sound rules, especially the "x" and the "tl." The borrowed letters mislead the stress guess. Once the syllables ACK-suh-lot-ul are fixed, the word is simple.

How do you pronounce axolotl in Spanish?

In Spanish, the related word ajolote is ah-ho-LO-teh, with the stress on the LO. Spanish stress rules put the weight on the second-to-last syllable. English "axolotl" follows the Nahuatl-derived spelling and stresses the first syllable instead.

Say ACK-suh-lot-ul and the word behaves like a simple four-syllable name. The spelling looks foreign because it is; the sound is easy once the stress lands on ACK.
