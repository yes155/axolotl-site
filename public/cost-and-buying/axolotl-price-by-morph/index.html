How much does an axolotl cost by morph?

A pet-quality axolotl costs $30-$150, from about $25 for common morphs to $1,000+ for rare ones. Wild type and leucistic morphs start around $25, mid-tier morphs run $60-$150, and chimera and mosaic animals climb into the hundreds. The table below shows realistic 2026 price ranges for the nine major morphs.

MorphTypical price rangeWhy the price varies
Wild type$25-$50Most common; plain brown and gray coloring
Leucistic$25-$60Very common white with pink gills
Melanoid$40-$80Dark morph, kept in lower numbers
Golden albino$40-$90Popular, in steady supply
Copper$60-$150Uncommon tan and copper tone
GFP$70-$150Glows under blue light; pricier to source
Piebald$100-$300Spotty pattern; variable supply
Mosaic$100-$400Patchy coloring; rare and sought after
Chimera$200-$1,000+Split-bodied; extremely rare

Every price in the table is a rarity label, not a health certificate, so the same morph can cost more or less depending on the breeder.

Why do some morphs cost more than others?

Price tracks three factors: rarity, demand, and breeding difficulty. Common morphs are easy to breed in volume, so supply keeps their prices at $25-$60. Rare morphs like chimeras occur only by chance, and stacked-gene morphs need multi-generation programs, which is why they command $150-$1,000+.

What is the cheapest axolotl morph?

Wild type and leucistic are the cheapest morphs, starting around $25. Both are common and produced in volume, so healthy juveniles stay at the bottom of the price scale. Melanoid and golden albino sit one step higher at $40-$90.

Are cheap axolotls a good deal?

Not always. Axolotls under $20 are usually too-young babies, rehoming listings, or a warning sign. Healthy, well-raised young axolotls from a reputable breeder rarely sell below $25. An unusually low price on a rare morph deserves extra scrutiny, because a healthy rare morph costs $150 or more.

What does an axolotl really cost to set up?

The animal is the smallest part of the bill: a full tank setup runs $150-$400, and monthly upkeep adds $20-$50. Shipping adds $30-$60 when the axolotl is not local. Budget for the habitat first, because the setup, not the animal, is where the money goes.

How do I find a fair price?

Compare prices across several breeders, judge the animal's health and size, and factor in $30-$60 of shipping. A healthy axolotl from a good breeder is worth more than a cheap one from an unknown source. A $25 wild type from a careful breeder beats a $25 leucistic from a seller who cannot show the parents.

Why are axolotls so expensive?

Rare morphs cost $150-$1,000+ because of genetics and breeding difficulty, while common morphs stay at $25-$60 because they are easy to breed in volume.

How much does a rare morph cost?

$150-$1,000+, with chimera and mosaic animals often clearing $300.

How much does a baby axolotl cost?

The same as an adult of the same morph, $25-$60 for common morphs, because breeders price by morph rather than by age.

How much does axolotl shipping add?

$30-$60 on top of the purchase price for an overnight live-arrival delivery.

A price tag on an axolotl is a rarity label, not a health certificate, so the breeder's husbandry matters more than the morph's price.
