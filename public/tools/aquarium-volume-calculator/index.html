Aquarium Volume Calculator

Enter the tank's inside length, inside width, and actual water depth for the best estimate. The calculator converts the rectangular water column into US gallons, liters, Imperial gallons and water weight, then applies an optional displacement allowance for substrate and décor.

Enter aquarium dimensions

Use 0% for an empty rectangular water column. Around 5–15% can be a reasonable planning allowance for ordinary décor, but measuring actual filled water is more accurate.

Formula

Inches: length × width × water depth ÷ 231 = US gallons. Centimeters: length × width × water depth ÷ 1,000 = liters. The “estimated usable water” result then subtracts your selected displacement percentage.

For a real aquarium, inside dimensions and actual fill depth are better inputs than the box's outside dimensions or the manufacturer's nominal gallon name. Glass thickness, frame height, air gap, substrate and décor all reduce actual water volume.

The dimensions below are Aqueon item dimensions from its 2025 catalog and are useful for footprint planning. They are not a substitute for measuring your aquarium's inside water column. Other manufacturers can differ.
