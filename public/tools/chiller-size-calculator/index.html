How the estimator works

The calculator starts with published reservoir-capacity and flow ranges for common compressor chiller classes, then adds headroom when the tank is near the top of a published range, the required temperature drop is large, or the heat load is high. It does not pretend that horsepower alone is an engineering heat-load calculation.
