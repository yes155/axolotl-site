The LEGO Minecraft The Axolotl House (set 21247) is the only dedicated axolotl LEGO set, a 242-piece buildable base shaped like Minecraft's pink salamander. This guide covers its specs, value, retirement, and alternatives.

Is There a LEGO Axolotl Set?

Yes. LEGO Minecraft The Axolotl House (set 21247) is the dedicated axolotl LEGO set, a 242-piece buildable axolotl-shaped base for ages 7 and up. It includes an underwater explorer, a drowned zombie, 2 adult axolotls, an axolotl in a bucket, a dolphin, and a pufferfish. It retailed at $26.99 when it launched in June 2023.

What Are the Specs of the LEGO Axolotl House?

The Axolotl House has 242 pieces, a 7+ age rating, and a $26.99 retail price. The full spec list:

What is in the box:

  • A giant axolotl-shaped house you can defend
  • 6 characters: an underwater explorer, a drowned zombie, 2 adult axolotls, a dolphin, and a pufferfish, plus an axolotl in a bucket
  • A hidden treasure chest with treasure
  • A lift-off roof that reveals a furnished interior with a smelting furnace and a sponge

The set works with the LEGO Builder app, which lets builders zoom in and rotate the model in 3D.

Is the Axolotl House Retired?

Yes. LEGO retired the Axolotl House from its retail run in December 2024, after a 1-year, 6-month shelf life. LEGO Shop US still lists it at $26.99, frequently discounted to $21.59. Sealed copies sell for $41-60 on the secondary market, and complete used sets average $24-42.

What Is the Value of the LEGO Axolotl House?

The Axolotl House cost $26.99 retail, which is 11 cents per piece, and sealed copies now average about $41-60 on the secondary market. Brick Ranker tracks the average sale price at $55.08 since retirement, up 104 percent from retail. On Bricklink, sealed copies average $40.96 and used copies average $24.46.

What Other LEGO Sets Have Axolotls?

Beyond set 21247, axolotls appear in LEGO Minecraft ocean-biome sets, but the Axolotl House is the only dedicated axolotl build. Minecraft added axolotls as a mob in 2021, and LEGO Minecraft followed with wave releases. Combine the Axolotl House with other Minecraft water sets, because the brand designs them to snap together.

Is the LEGO Axolotl House Worth It?

Yes. At $26.99 for 242 pieces, the Axolotl House is a solid value for ages 7 and up. The price per piece of about 11 cents is average for Minecraft sets. It pairs the collectible axolotl minifigs with a playable base, which makes it a safe gift for any Minecraft or axolotl fan.

How Much Is the LEGO Minecraft Axolotl House?

The Axolotl House retailed at $26.99 in the US, and LEGO Shop US still lists it at that price, frequently on sale for $21.59. Since retirement, sealed copies average $41-60 on the secondary market. Complete used sets average $24-42.

How Many Pieces Is the LEGO Axolotl Set?

The Axolotl House has 242 pieces, rated for ages 7 and up. It builds to about 4 inches high by 6.5 inches wide by 5.5 inches deep. The two minifigures, Drowned Zombie and Diver Explorer, add about $4.69 of collector value.

Why Is There a LEGO Axolotl?

Minecraft added axolotls as an aquatic mob in 2021, and LEGO Minecraft turned the popular creature into a full playset in 2023. The Axolotl House came out of Minecraft's wave-to-wave set releases. It remains one of the most searched Minecraft axolotl kits.
