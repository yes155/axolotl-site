The Complete Axolotl Squishmallow Guide

Axolotl Squishmallows are among the most collected plush in the world, and the most famous is Archie the Axolotl, released in 2019. This guide lists every named axolotl character, all the standard sizes, retail prices, and the rules for buying without overpaying.

Are There Real Axolotl Squishmallows?

Yes. The first axolotl Squishmallow, Archie the Axolotl, was released in 2019, and collectors have catalogued more than 200 named axolotl characters since. Jazwares gives every character a name, a bio, and a collection number. Axolotls span squads including Sealife, Valentine, Easter, and Day of the Dead.

What Is the Rarest Axolotl Squishmallow?

The rarest axolotl Squishmallows are seasonal and store-exclusive prints, including certain Easter and Valentine characters, jumbo 24-inch versions, and older Archie prints. Rarity shifts with each release. A Party City Archie in 24-inch and a Five Below Heart Ice Cream Archie in 8-inch are among the hardest to find.

What Sizes Do Axolotl Squishmallows Come In?

Axolotl Squishmallows come in sizes from a 2.5-inch micromallow to a 24-inch jumbo, across 11 standard plush sizes per Jazwares. The sizes are 3.5-inch clips, 5, 7, 8, 11, 12, 13, 14, 16, 20, and 24 inches, with the 13-inch size discontinued. The 8-inch and 12-inch versions are the most common gifts.

  • 3.5-inch Clip: keychain size for backpacks, $7-10
  • 5-inch: palm-sized for kids and desks, $10-15
  • 8-inch: the classic first Squishmallow, $15-20
  • 12-inch: the collector's favorite cuddle size, $20-30
  • 14 and 16-inch: big lounging companions, $30-50
  • 20 and 24-inch: jumbo, sold mainly at Costco and big-box stores, $40-60+

Check the size on the tag: the number after the 'S' is the size in inches, so 'S8' means an 8-inch plush.

What Are the Names of Axolotl Squishmallows?

The most wanted axolotl Squishmallow names are Archie, Aldea, Aleka, Anastasia, Irina, Miss Vi, and Siobhan. New characters launch constantly, especially around Valentine's Day and Easter, which are the two big seasonal windows. The catalogued list:

How Much Does an Axolotl Squishmallow Cost?

At retail, axolotl Squishmallows cost $7-10 for clips, $10-15 for 5-inch, $15-20 for 8-inch, $20-30 for 12-inch, $30-50 for 14-16-inch, and $40-60+ for jumbos. A 16-inch axolotl sells for $12.99 online at Costco, which is cheaper than most retailers. A 12-inch axolotl for far under $15 is counterfeit.

How Do You Tell a Real Axolotl Squishmallow from a Fake?

A real axolotl Squishmallow has a printed hang tag and a sewn butt tag with the name, squad, and made-in info clearly printed. These 5 checks catch counterfeits:

  • Check the printed hang tag and sewn butt tag for the name, squad, and made-in info
  • Feel the fabric: genuine minky feels marshmallow-soft, fakes feel rougher and stiffer
  • Buy from authorized retailers: Target, Costco, Walmart, Amazon, Aldi, and the Jazwares store
  • Compare the price: a 12-inch axolotl for far under $15 is counterfeit
  • Check the bio card or tag with the character's full name and number

How Do You Buy Axolotl Squishmallows Without Overpaying?

Buy at retail from authorized stores instead of resellers, and you pay about half the resale price. These 5 rules save money:

  1. Wait for the big-box drops at Target, Costco, and Aldi, which sell themed squads at retail prices
  1. Buy seasonal early: Valentine's and Easter axolotls sell out, but retail prices hold in the first weeks
  1. Set a maximum price for each scale: clips $8, 5-inch $12, 8-inch $18, 12-inch $25
  1. Use the official app and Jazwares shop for restock alerts on rare Archie prints
  1. Check local trade pages, because axolotl collectors swap duplicates

How Big Is the Axolotl Squishmallow?

Axolotl Squishmallows range from a 2.5-inch micromallow to a 24-inch jumbo, and Archie alone comes in 8 sizes from 3.5 to 24 inches. The 8-inch and 12-inch versions are the most common gifts. The 16-inch versions sell for $12.99 online at Costco.

How Much Is a Squishmallow Archie?

Archie retails at $7-10 for a clip, $10-15 for 5-inch, $15-20 for 8-inch, and $20-30 for 12-inch. Resale prices for sold-out prints run much higher. Costco sells 16-inch versions for $12.99 online.

Are There Blue Axolotl Squishmallows?

Yes. Jazwares has released axolotl characters in blue, purple, rainbow, galaxy, and maroon, alongside the classic pink. Irina is a teal axolotl, and IndieMae is a maroon axolotl with a fuzzy belly. Check the character's color before buying.
