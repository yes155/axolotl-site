Build-A-Bear Workshop sells the Pink Axolotl for $32, and the axolotl has been a best-selling furry friend since it debuted in 2021. This guide covers every model, the current prices, and the steps to customize one.

Does Build-A-Bear Sell an Axolotl?

Yes. Build-A-Bear sells the Pink Axolotl Stuffed Animal for $32, and the axolotl has been a best seller since the first one debuted in 2021. The line grew to include a Lavender Axolotl in April 2023, a Zombie Axolotl for Halloween, a Gingerbread Axolotl for Christmas, and Mini Beans. The Pink Axolotl was a Toy of the Year finalist.

What Build-A-Bear Axolotls Exist?

Build-A-Bear sells 7 axolotl products, from a $10 Mini Beans to a $42 gift set. The full lineup with current prices:

A Lavender Axolotl joined the line on April 17, 2023, and a Pineapple Axolotl launched at $32 in the 2025 Fruit Stand collection.

What Is the Best Build-A-Bear Axolotl?

The Pink Axolotl is the best build-a-bear axolotl, because it is the original 8-inch design at the standard $32 price. It has a wide cheerful smile, signature gills, and pebbly fabric that survives years of use. It is the version most people picture when they say 'Build-A-Bear axolotl'.

How Do You Customize a Build-A-Bear Axolotl?

A full custom build pairs the $32 axolotl with a sound chip, a scent, and an outfit. The 5 steps:

  1. Choose the plush: the Pink Axolotl or a seasonal edition
  1. Add a sound chip, $8-10, with pre-recorded messages or a 20-second personal recording
  1. Add a scent disc, $4-5, in flavors like strawberry or birthday cake
  1. Dress it: pajamas to graduation gowns, with shoes because the axolotl 'walks'
  1. Get a birth certificate with a name and wish, included in most builds

You can customize online through the Bear Builder or in the in-store Workshop experience.

How Much Does a Build-A-Bear Axolotl Cost?

A base Build-A-Bear axolotl costs $32, and a fully customized one costs $55-85. The breakdown:

Mini Beans axolotls cost $10, and the Pink Axolotl + Mini Beans gift set costs $42.

Is the Build-A-Bear Axolotl Good for Kids?

Yes. The Build-A-Bear axolotl is soft, well-made, and fully customizable, and the Workshop experience suits kids aged 3 and up. The 8-inch size fits small hands. The 20-second voice recording makes it a keepsake that kids remember.

How Do You Avoid Fake Build-A-Bear Axolotls?

Buy from buildabear.com or a Workshop to avoid fakes, because the axolotl's popularity attracts knockoffs on marketplaces. These rules help:

  • Order seasonal editions early: the Valentine's and Halloween axolotls sell out fast
  • Use Mini Beans as budget gifts, because they bundle small for $10
  • Add the sound chip and scent for a custom gift, because kids remember the personalized experience

How Much Is a Build-A-Bear Axolotl?

The plush itself is $32, a fully customized axolotl costs $55-85, and a Mini Beans axolotl costs $10. The Pink Axolotl + Mini Beans gift set costs $42. Seasonal editions hold the same base price as the standard plush.

Can You Record a Message in a Build-A-Bear Axolotl?

Yes. You can add a sound chip with a 20-second voice recording, recorded online or in the Workshop. The chip costs $8-10. You can also add a scent and outfits.

Is There a Build-A-Bear Pink Axolotl?

Yes. The signature axolotl is the Pink Axolotl at $32, with a smiling face and feathery gills. Sparkly pink 'Love You Alotl' versions appear around Valentine's Day. A Lavender Axolotl joined the line on April 17, 2023.
